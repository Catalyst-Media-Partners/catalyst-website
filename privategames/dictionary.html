A bigger word library.

Our bundled dictionary contains 62,368 playable English words with definitions, drawn from Princeton WordNet. It works without a separate dictionary service during play.

Choose your vocabulary

LevelWordsHow it is selected
Easy2,222Estimated common vocabulary (wordfreq Zipf frequency 4.5 or higher).
Middle school6,997Estimated moderate vocabulary (Zipf 3.5–4.49), plus the original 50 curated words; not a certified grade list.
Advanced22,965Estimated less-common vocabulary (Zipf 2.0–3.49); includes technical terms.
Full dictionary62,368All playable entries in the bundled dictionary, including rare and technical words; names, nonalphabetic entries and filtered senses are excluded.

Difficulty is estimated from how frequently words appear in the wordfreq corpus. These are familiarity bands, not certified school reading grades. A common word can still be difficult to spell, and a longer word can still be familiar.

What “Full dictionary” includes

All eligible entries in this bundled corpus, including rare and technical vocabulary. Words must contain 3–15 English letters. Names, phrases, hyphenated words, and selected unsuitable headwords and definitions are excluded. The filter is intended for a family game; the corpus has not been manually reviewed word by word for classroom use.

You can select any minimum and maximum length from 3 to 15 letters. The game shows how many words match and prevents starting with an empty selection. It remembers the latest 100 words to reduce repeats, relaxing the oldest exclusions when a small selection runs out.

Definitions and sources

Definitions are adapted from Princeton WordNet 3.0. Examples are removed, occurrences of the answer are replaced with blanks, and longer definitions are shortened. The original 50 hand-written clues remain available in Middle school.

Frequency estimates come from wordfreq 3.2 by Robyn Speer. The adapted dictionary data is offered under CC BY-SA 4.0, with the original WordNet notice retained. This data license does not relicense the game code.
