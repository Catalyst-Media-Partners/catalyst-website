Fulfillment and delivery policy

Catalyst provides exhibit booth space and event-related sponsorship, advertising, registration and media services. No physical retail goods are sold or shipped through this website.

  • Exhibit space is fulfilled through written space confirmation, floor-plan assignment and exhibitor onboarding for the named event.
  • Sponsorship, advertising and media services are fulfilled through the placements, recognition, content or distribution deliverables stated in the applicable order.
  • Registration is fulfilled through electronic confirmation, credential issuance or on-site check-in access for the named event.
  • Customer-supplied content, artwork and approvals must be delivered by stated deadlines. Missed deadlines can reduce or prevent fulfillment without creating a refund right.

Business scope: Catalyst does not sell, manufacture, distribute, broker, facilitate or participate in any sales or revenue from cannabis, hemp, THC, CBD, nicotine, controlled substances or other regulated product inventory; Catalyst revenue is limited to trade-show exhibit space and related sponsorship, advertising, registration and media services.

Any third-party venue, freight, electrical, internet, labor, hotel or travel services are separate unless expressly included in the Catalyst order.
